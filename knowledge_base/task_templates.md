# Task Templates

Non-medical task patterns PawPal AI can propose. Durations are typical
defaults; the owner's request always wins when it specifies a value.

## Walk

- Applies to: dogs
- Typical duration: 30 minutes
- Typical priority: high (dogs need daily exercise)
- Typical frequency: daily

## Feeding

- Applies to: all pets
- Typical duration: 10 minutes for dogs, 5 minutes for cats
- Typical priority: high
- Typical frequency: daily
- Note: when a walk and a feeding are requested together in the morning,
  schedule the walk first (see owner preferences)

## Water Refill

- Applies to: all pets
- Typical duration: 5 minutes
- Typical priority: medium
- Typical frequency: daily

## Enrichment

- Applies to: all pets
- Examples: fetch, puzzle feeder, feather wand play
- Typical duration: 15 minutes
- Typical priority: medium
- Typical frequency: daily

## Grooming

- Applies to: all pets
- Examples: brushing, nail trim
- Typical duration: 15-20 minutes
- Typical priority: low
- Typical frequency: weekly

## Litter Box Cleaning

- Applies to: cats
- Typical duration: 10 minutes
- Typical priority: medium
- Typical frequency: daily
- Usually scheduled in the evening

## Vet Appointment Reminder

- Applies to: all pets
- Typical duration: 60 minutes
- Typical priority: high
- Typical frequency: once
- The system only records the reminder; the vet provides all medical care

## Prescribed Medication Reminder

- Applies to: pets with an EXISTING veterinarian-prescribed routine
- Typical duration: 5 minutes
- Typical priority: high
- The system may schedule a routine the owner already knows
  (for example "give Biscuit his heartworm pill on the 1st of the month")
- The system must NOT select medication
- The system must NOT determine dosage
- The system must NOT diagnose symptoms
