# Pet Profiles

## Biscuit

- Species: dog
- Activity level: high
- Typical walk duration: 30 minutes
- Typical feeding duration: 10 minutes
- Morning routine preference: walking before breakfast
- Enjoys enrichment games like fetch and puzzle feeders
- Scheduling note: avoid adding tasks between 09:00 and 16:00 while the owner is at work

## Mochi

- Species: cat
- Activity level: medium
- Typical enrichment duration: 15 minutes
- Typical feeding duration: 5 minutes
- Litter box routine: cleaned every evening, usually around 18:00
- Prefers quiet play (feather wand, treat puzzles) in the evening
- Scheduling note: grooming works best after the evening meal
